## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
i)Our proposed solution is to create awareness about mobile gaming and encourage people to play games in a balanced way.
ii)Mobile games are very popular among students and youngsters. They are useful for entertainment and sometimes help to improve thinking skills. But playing games for too much time can affect studies and daily activities.
iii)Our idea is to promote healthy gaming habits by following simple steps like setting time limits, taking breaks and choosing good games. This will help students enjoy gaming without affecting their studies.

## Technical Approach
 We can use HTML, CSS to create a simple website about mobile gaming.
 The website can include information about different types of games, their advantages and disadvantages.
 We can also add a gaming time reminder to help users control their playing time.
 The website will be simple and easy for everyone to use.

## Feasibility and Viability
i)This idea is possible to develop because we can use basic programming languages and easily available tools.
ii) Some users may not follow the time limits.
Developing all features may take more time.
We may face some technical problems while creating the website.
iii) Give regular reminders to users.
Develop the website step by step.
Test the website and correct the errors.


## Impact and Benefits
i)Our project can help students understand both the good and bad effects of mobile gaming.
ii)Helps students manage their gaming time.
Creates awareness about gaming addiction.
Encourages students to focus on their studies.
Helps users understand online gaming safety.
Promotes a healthy balance between gaming and daily life
